One More Pass — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026 · Applies to the One More Pass app for Android (gg.onemorepass.game) and the browser version.

Summary

One More Pass does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (such as language, sound and accessibility options) are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The Android app does not request any runtime permissions and does not use the internet permission.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. If you download the game from Google Play, Google may process data under its own privacy policy; that processing is outside the game's control.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version adds an online feature), this page will be updated and the effective date changed before that version is released.
